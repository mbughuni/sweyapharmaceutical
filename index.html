      About Sweya Pharmaceutical Limited

      Sweya Pharmaceutical Limited is a pharmaceutical and healthcare company based in Mwanza, Tanzania, engaged in the manufacturing and supply of pharmaceutical products, medical supplies and healthcare equipment for hospitals, clinics and healthcare institutions. Our goal is to support reliable access to quality healthcare products through professional, quality-focused manufacturing and supply.
